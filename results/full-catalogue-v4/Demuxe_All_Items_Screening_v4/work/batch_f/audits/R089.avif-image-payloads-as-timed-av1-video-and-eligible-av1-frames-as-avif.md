35: ## R89 — AVIF payloads as timed AV1 video — PROMISING
36: 
37: Three independently encoded 320×180 AVIF stills (red/green/blue) were fed through FFmpeg's concat demuxer and packet-copied into MP4. The first three AV1 packet SHA-256 hashes are **identical** to the respective AVIF packet hashes. Chrome presented the matching colors at 0.5/1.5/2.5 s, supported seek and EOF.
38: 
39: The reverse direction also worked: the green MP4 sample was packet-copied into AVIF and retained the exact compressed packet SHA-256. Chrome decoded the original and reconstructed AVIF to the same center pixel `[1,128,2,255]`.
40: 
41: This is a real compressed-representation crossover: compatible AV1 coded images can become timed media, and eligible independent AV1 samples can become AVIF, without a pixel round trip.
