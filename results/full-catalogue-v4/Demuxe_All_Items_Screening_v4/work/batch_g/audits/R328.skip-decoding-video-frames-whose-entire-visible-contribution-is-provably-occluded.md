Bounded next test and falsifier: Define a source-time opaque-coverage contract and controlled no-b-pyramid reference oracle; test one nonreference covered picture and reject covered reference/partial-alpha cases. Count modest historical savings honestly.

## R328 — skip fully occluded non-reference pictures
