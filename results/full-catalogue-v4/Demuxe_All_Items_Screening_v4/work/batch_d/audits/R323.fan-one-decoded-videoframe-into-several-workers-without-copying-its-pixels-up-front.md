## First-pass decision

Hypothesis: The proposed mechanism 'Fan one decoded VideoFrame into several workers without copying its pixels up front' may provide the specified output with less work or add a faithful route for its stated profile.

Contract: Each independent consumer gets correct frame/time resource reference with bounded slow-consumer retention and complete release.

Current gap: Current output has one transferred owner; there is no multi-consumer accounting/deadline policy. Cloning frames without bounded fanout can retain decoder surfaces indefinitely.

Cheapest correct baseline: Independent explicitly bounded consumer copies of one decoded frame.

Primary outcome: Correct additional admitted source/destination capability; otherwise full startup and CPU/resource cost. No numerical outcome was measured.

Bounded next test and falsifier: Define a two-consumer frame lease with one slow/canceled worker, clone resource references, and prove source replacement closes all remaining owners; count peak retained surfaces.
