## R137 — transferable compressed buffers into worker MSE

Even though the worker MSE session could not open, the ownership primitive is decisive. Three compressed buffers transferred main→worker changed from **777, 90,419 and 96,393 bytes** to **0-byte detached ArrayBuffers** on the sender immediately after `postMessage(..., [buffer])`.

That establishes a real no-retained-main-copy handoff primitive. The missing evidence is whether those transferred bytes can be appended and played in the same worker-owned MSE route under a qualified origin.

## First-pass decision

Hypothesis: The proposed mechanism 'transferable compressed buffers into worker MSE' may provide the specified output with less work or add a faithful route for its stated profile.

Contract: Owned compressed ArrayBuffer detaches on transfer and reaches actual worker SourceBuffer output with no premature release or hidden fallback.

Current gap: Compressed buffers already transfer mux-worker to window; moving MSE would change final ownership, not eliminate FFmpeg-to-owned buffer copy. Worker attachment is the missing end-to-end primitive.

Cheapest correct baseline: Existing worker-to-window transfer and window SourceBuffer append.

Primary outcome: User-visible operation latency, duplicated work or peak/steady live resource ownership; not object counts alone. No numerical outcome was measured.

Bounded next test and falsifier: After valid worker-MSE attachment, transfer one owned compressed buffer, assert sender detachment and actual decoded output; cancel before append and prove release without publication.

Reopen only when the named missing contract/owner exists, a concrete workload exposes removable work, or the stated next slice changes this finding. A historical report assertion is not a newly verified current result.

Limits: per-mechanism source screen only; code/test presence does not prove successful runtime. No generic family verdict or missing-source inference was used.
