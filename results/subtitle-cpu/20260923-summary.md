## Route and fixture contract

All five 36 s synthetic stereo fixtures contain 320 × 180 H.264 and AAC with one embedded target subtitle. Exact SHA-256 values and paths are in the raw reports. Automatic selection picked `native-direct-mpv` in every accepted trial. Browser media element owns video and audio; mpv's subtitle-only service owns subtitle demux and rendering; full Hybrid was not used. The Native + mpv service reported zero A/V chains on both lanes. Each trial had one subtitle worker, one I/O worker and four subtitle Wasm pthread workers; subtitle Wasm heap was 67,108,864 bytes. The selected codec was, respectively, `subrip`, `mov_text`, `ass`, `hdmv_pgs_subtitle`, and `dvd_subtitle`.

## Test-only cadence ablation

The experiment served a modified generated host module that scheduled the next subtitle tick at about 10 Hz instead of each animation frame. It never changed source or generated files on disk. In three accepted SRT pairs, render calls fell from about 60/s to 9.8/s. Median renderer on/off delta fell from +3.0 to approximately 0 points. ASS's three accepted ablation pairs likewise had about 9.8 calls/s and a median renderer delta of +0.3 points, versus +6.7 at baseline. SRT whole-Chrome paired deltas were `-3.5,+1.1,+13.5` points, so the whole-player saving is not established. A blanket 10 Hz rule is not correctness-qualified for animated ASS/karaoke or fast cues and must not be shipped.

## Correctness and limits

Steady CPU trials required matched route, selected AAC, unmuted media element, media-time progression, at least 20 decoded video frames/s, at most five dropped frames, and zero mpv A/V chains. The first campaign lacked an audio byte counter; the interrupted confirmation added one and observed positive, similar AAC decoded-byte deltas in its completed SRT lanes. Visual cue assertions were performed separately with the existing direct-route generalization test: SRT and mov_text text, ASS magenta styling, PGS/VobSub palette and transparency, active and blank seek positions all reached their assertions. The suite was interrupted after those outputs, so its later selection, pause, visibility and teardown stages are not counted as a full pass. A separate continuous PGS playback check showed a bitmap present from about 6–12 s where the existing paused-seek test expects none at 6 s. This discrepancy needs an oracle before claiming PGS seek-recovery fidelity. Animated ASS, source replacement, resize, and complete teardown were not qualified by this CPU experiment.

## Decision

The only measured optimization candidate is **avoiding host-to-worker render RPCs during intervals known to have unchanged output**, with explicit animation and cue-boundary handling. It needs an uncontended whole-player rerun and correctness qualification before production work. Do not implement ordinary text-extraction removal, geometry caching, cropped bitmap transport, BGRA lookup conversion, or bitmap composition changes based on this campaign: their steady whole-player contribution is either below the noise floor or absent in these windows. Do not optimize libass specifically: this fixture did not show it dominating total CPU.
