The complete `demuxe` package is GPL-3.0-or-later. The separately assembled `demuxe-core`
package contains only the explicit reusable source list and its compiled output.
Its original software is Apache-2.0 only. The GPLv3 player includes Apache modules
under their compatible Apache terms, retaining their license and notices.
The Apache grant does not extend to the complete player or linked GPL engines.

See [the full licensing and distribution policy](docs/LICENSING.md),
[machine-checked boundaries](licensing/boundaries.json),
[third-party notices](third_party/notices.json), and
[media attribution](docs/MEDIA-NOTICES.md). License texts are in `LICENSES/`.
The manifest annotates non-commentable files and preserved research evidence;
it does not override third-party or historical source notices.
