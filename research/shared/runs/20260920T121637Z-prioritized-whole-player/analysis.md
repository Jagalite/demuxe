# Prioritized whole-player route qualification

Two most promising ready subtitle substitutions were measured before expensive new player mechanisms. Five alternating pairs per route, declared primary CPU gate, matching frozen correctness and lifecycle proofs.

Results and precise tier owners are in results.json. These are complete player experiments in authored fixtures, not isolated decoder/render loops. Browser launch, external media services and server CPU are excluded and openly reported. Startup, steady CPU, summed RSS, main-thread work, seeks and player lifecycle observations are retained; CPU saving is not energy proof.

R041 additionally depends on bounded embedded SRT extraction; R019 uses the combined extractor plus independent renderer. Do not double-count a shared route gain as independent mechanism savings. Production automatic admission remains unchanged; bounded fixtures do not qualify arbitrary inputs or production extraction cancellation and range budgets.
